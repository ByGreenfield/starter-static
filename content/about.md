---
heading: About the bakery
---

We opened in 2019 with one oven and a sourdough starter named Earl. Today we bake for the neighborhood every morning, supply three coffee shops, and make cakes to order for birthdays and weddings.

Everything is made from scratch. The flour comes from a mill in the Hill Country, the butter is from a dairy in Dripping Springs, and the fruit changes with the season.

Come by early for the best selection. We usually sell out of croissants by eleven.
